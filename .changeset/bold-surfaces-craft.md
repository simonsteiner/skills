New model-invoked `frontend-craft` skill for designing and building UI with a point of view instead of templated defaults. It picks the surface's mode (Persuade, Operate, Read, Experience), plans a token system and reviews it against the brief before building, builds to a quality floor, and checks the render in bounded passes. It also critiques and audits existing UI and handles bolder, quieter, simpler, and polish requests. Adapted from Anthropic's `frontend-design` and Paul Bakaus's `impeccable`, without impeccable's engine, hooks, or project files.
