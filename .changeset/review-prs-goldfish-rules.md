review-prs paraphrases what a diff does from the code alone and flags a PR body that claims more, or less, than the diff shows. It searches the repo for an existing helper before accepting a new one, reports a review sub-agent that fails twice as not reviewed instead of clean, and drops one of its own findings only with evidence a reader can check. Adapted from Google's Elephant-Goldfish Model.
