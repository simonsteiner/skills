# Credits

The *Disagree with evidence* rules in Step 2 are ideas from the resolve skill of the Elephant-Goldfish Model, <https://github.com/google/elephant-goldfish-model> (Apache-2.0), reworded; no text is carried over: a disagree cites something the reviewer can open, a reviewer's misreading still gets a clarity fix, a finding is fixed as raised or disagreed with out loud, and the author's wish isn't evidence.
